# Data Engineering Communities – Frankfurt (Networking + Learning)

## 🔥 Top Data Engineering Communities (Frankfurt)

### 1. Frankfurt Data Engineering Meetup
- Most relevant community for **Data Engineers / Cloud Engineers**
- Regular meetups, talks, and real industry use cases  
- Great for **networking + job opportunities**

👉 https://www.meetup.com/frankfurt-data-engineering/

---

### 2. Frankfurt Data Streaming Meetup
- Focus: **Kafka, Flink, Real-time pipelines**
- Covers **streaming systems + Dataflow concepts**

👉 https://www.meetup.com/frankfurt-flink/

---

### 3. Data & AI Meetup Frankfurt
- Covers **Data Engineering + AI + Analytics**
- Industry exposure and company-level discussions  

👉 https://www.infomotion.de/events/

---

### 4. Databricks User Group Frankfurt
- Focus: **Apache Spark, Databricks, Big Data platforms**
- Strong **industry networking + hiring exposure**

👉 https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=Databricks%20Frankfurt%20User%20Group

---

### 5. Cloud Native Frankfurt
- Focus: **Kubernetes, Cloud Infrastructure, DevOps**
- Important for **modern data engineering ecosystems**

👉 https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-frankfurt/

---

## 🌍 Global / Online Communities

### Reddit – Data Engineering
- Global discussions, real-world problems, solutions  
- Good for **learning + freelance exposure**

## 🎯 Practical Strategy

1. Join **Meetup.com** and relevant Frankfurt groups  
2. Attend **at least 1 event per month**  
3. Connect with people on **LinkedIn after events**  
4. Talk to **speakers and organizers**

- Learn **real company use cases**
