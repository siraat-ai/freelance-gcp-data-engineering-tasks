# 🇩🇪 Germany Skilled Labor Shortage – Notes (BBC Urdu)

🔗 **Source Article:**
https://www.bbc.com/urdu/articles/clydzn1mdlmo

---

## 📌 Overview

Germany is facing a **severe shortage of skilled workers** due to:

* 👴 Aging population (retirement increase)
* 👶 Low birth rate
* 📉 Fewer young workers entering the workforce

👉 As a result, Germany is now **actively hiring talent from abroad**.

---

## 🚨 Key Statistics

* 📊 Germany needs **~288,000 foreign workers annually**
* 📉 Workforce could shrink by **10% by 2024**
* 🇮🇳 Indian workers in Germany:

  * 2015: **23,320**
  * 2024: **136,670** ⬆️

---

## 🌍 Main Strategy: Foreign Recruitment

### 🇮🇳 Focus on India

* Huge young population:

  * 👥 **60 million under age 25**
* Limited local job opportunities:

  * 💼 Only **12 million jobs/year**

👉 Germany sees India as a **key talent source**

---

## 🤝 Government Actions

### 📜 Migration Agreement

* 📅 Signed in **2022**
* 🇩🇪🇮🇳 **Migration & Mobility Partnership**

### 🛂 Visa Expansion

* Increased work visas for Indians:

  * ⬆️ From **20,000 → 90,000 per year**

---

## 🏢 Role of Private Agencies

### 🔹 “Magic Billion”

* Recruited Indian youth for training in Germany

### 🔹 “India Works”

* Founded by Hendrik Wann
* هدف:

  * Import skilled workers from India
* 📅 2025 target: **775 workers**

---

## 🛠️ Affected Industries

### 🔴 High Shortage Areas:

* 🥩 Butchers (قصاب)
* 🍞 Bakery
* 🔧 Mechanics
* 🚧 Road construction

👉 Example:

* Butcher shops declined:

  * 2002: **19,000**
  * 2021: **11,000**

## 🏙️ Local Impact in Germany

* 🏪 Businesses saved by foreign workers
* 🧑‍🏫 Teacher shortages unresolved locally
* 🏛️ Officials say:

  > “We must look outside the country — it’s the only solution”
